Our Mission

No one should die waiting for blood.

Every year, thousands of preventable deaths occur in Cameroon because hospitals run out of blood and patients can't find donors in time. LifeDrop was built to solve this.

We connect eligible blood donors with hospitals and recipients in real time — eliminating the information gap, reducing delays, and making blood donation data-driven and efficient.

From emergency alerts to smart donor matching, every feature is designed with one mission in mind: save more lives, faster.
