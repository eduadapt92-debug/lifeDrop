For Blood Donors
Who can donate blood?▼
Anyone aged 17–65 who weighs at least 50kg, is generally healthy, and has not donated in the last 56 days (8 weeks). Certain medical conditions may temporarily or permanently disqualify you — the platform will guide you through a self-assessment when you register.
How often can I donate?▼
Whole blood: once every 56 days (8 weeks). Platelets: every 7 days (up to 24 times/year). Plasma: every 28 days. LifeDrop tracks your last donation date and will notify you when you're eligible again.
Will I get notified when someone needs my blood group?▼
Yes! When a blood request matching your blood group is created in your city, you'll receive an instant push notification (and email if configured). You can choose to accept or decline the request from your dashboard.
What is the Donor Tier system?▼
LifeDrop rewards frequent donors with tier recognition: Bronze (1–4 donations), Silver (5–9), Gold (10–19), Platinum (20+). Higher tiers earn more points, priority visibility in the donor matching system, and digital certificates of recognition.
Is my personal data safe?▼
Absolutely. Your personal data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. Hospitals only see your blood group and general location — never your full personal details — until you accept a donation request.
For Blood Recipients
How do I request blood urgently?▼
Go to the Emergency Request page and fill in the blood group, quantity, location, and urgency level. Your request will be broadcast immediately to eligible donors near you. No account required for emergency requests.
How long does it take to find a donor?▼
Our average response time is 45 minutes for critical requests in urban areas. Response times may vary depending on your location, the requested blood group, and donor availability. O- blood (universal donor) typically has the fastest response.
Is there a cost to request blood?▼
LifeDrop's matching service is completely free. Blood donation itself is voluntary and free by law. You may incur standard hospital fees for the collection and processing procedure, which vary by institution.
What if I don't know my blood group?▼
You can request any blood group or select "Unknown". The hospital will verify compatibility before transfusion. We recommend getting your blood group tested — any partner hospital on LifeDrop can do this quickly.
For Hospitals & Blood Banks
How does my hospital join the LifeDrop network?▼
Register on the registration page as a Hospital or Blood Bank. Upload your authorization documents. Our admin team will verify your institution within 48 hours and grant you full access to the Hospital Dashboard.
How does real-time inventory work?▼
Hospital staff update blood inventory directly from the dashboard. The system automatically calculates status (Critical/Low/Moderate/Stable/Optimal) and triggers alerts when stock falls below thresholds. Low-stock alerts notify admins and suggest donor outreach.
Can I notify specific donors directly?▼
Yes. The Donor Match feature lets you see eligible donors near your location and send them direct notifications from your dashboard. Donors who match your needed blood group and are within the 56-day eligibility window are surfaced first.
Is there an API for our existing hospital system?▼
Yes! LifeDrop offers a REST API that can integrate with existing hospital management systems. Contact us at api@lifedrop.com for API documentation and integration support.
Technical Questions
How does the blood type matching algorithm work?▼
Our algorithm considers ABO/Rh compatibility, donor eligibility (56-day rule), location proximity (city and area scoring), and donor availability status. Donors are ranked by compatibility score and recency of last donation.
Does LifeDrop work offline?▼
LifeDrop requires an internet connection for real-time features. Emergency requests, donor matching, and inventory updates require connectivity. We're working on a lightweight offline mode for areas with intermittent connectivity.
What devices are supported?▼
LifeDrop works on any modern browser including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. The interface is fully responsive and optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.
How are notifications sent?▼
Notifications are delivered via real-time WebSocket (Socket.IO) when you're online, and via email for offline users. SMS notifications are in development for areas with limited data connectivity.
